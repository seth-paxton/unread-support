Unread

Just get started. Unread helps you capture a thought, choose one task, and find a manageable next step on iPhone.

Capture and focus

Tap Capture to type, or hold it to speak. Voice capture saves after about four seconds of silence; you can switch to typing to make corrections. Share text or a link from another app to Unread, or use its Shortcuts actions.

Now keeps one task in your hand. Edit its next step, swap focus, try five minutes, or use Make it easier. Complete it when you choose. Undo is available briefly after completion, and completed tasks can be restored from Settings.

Unread and waiting

Active tasks come back into view after 24 hours without interaction. Their age is a memory cue, never a deadline. Mark a task as waiting when it depends on someone else, or let it go when you no longer need it.

Privacy and availability

Tasks are stored locally and sync through your private iCloud account when available. Widgets and the Share extension use the same app group. AI assistance requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware and model availability; you can write your own step without it. Voice capture needs microphone and speech-recognition permission and may require an internet connection.

Troubleshooting

If speech is unavailable, use typing and check Microphone & speech in Settings. For sync, check iCloud sign-in and the status shown in Unread Settings. Keep private task information out of screenshots sent to support.

Contact

For help, bug reports, feedback, or privacy inquiries, email sethpaxton@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version and a description of the problem. Please do not send private task content, recordings, passwords, or account credentials.
