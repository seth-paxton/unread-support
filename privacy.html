Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. Unread is published by Seth Paxton. Contact sethpaxton@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Task information and local storage

Unread stores the tasks you capture, context, original wording, next steps, shared links, creation/review/completion information, waiting state, and preferences on your device. It has no developer-operated account or server, advertising, tracking SDK, or third-party analytics service. The publisher does not receive task content from the app.

The app, its widgets and Share extension use a shared app group for task storage. If you use The Brief on the same device, that app can read the task/focus snapshot shared with Unread’s widgets. Focus choices and other local preferences remain on the device.

Private iCloud sync

When you are signed into iCloud and the service is available, task content and state sync through Apple CloudKit’s private database. Unread stores task payloads in encrypted CloudKit fields. The publisher cannot read your private database. Offline changes are queued for later sync; deletion markers help keep deleted tasks from returning from another device. Apple’s services follow Apple’s privacy policy.

Optional speech and microphone

Microphone and speech-recognition permission are requested when you choose voice capture. Audio is supplied to Apple’s Speech framework for transcription, which may use Apple’s servers depending on the device, language and service availability. Do not assume every transcription stays on the device. Unread does not retain an audio recording file or send recordings to the publisher. The resulting text is stored as task content. You can use typed capture instead and revoke permissions in iPhone Settings.

AI, spoken tasks and system integrations

Optional title refinement and next-step suggestions use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework on compatible devices. Unread does not send task prompts to an external AI provider. Suggestions can be wrong; next-step drafts are editable before you keep them. Manual task editing remains available without AI.

If enabled, the app can read a task aloud using Apple’s speech synthesis. Widgets, Shortcuts, Live Activities and notification/badge features may display task information on system surfaces. Choose their visibility and permissions in the app and system Settings, particularly on a shared or locked device. Shared text and URLs are saved only when you choose to save them; opening a saved link uses the destination service’s own policies.

Retention and your choices

Tasks, including completed tasks, remain until you remove them. Completing a task is not deletion. Let go of an active task or delete a completed task to remove it; sync retains deletion markers to prevent its return. Removing the app does not necessarily remove its iCloud records or shared app-group data used by remaining extensions/apps. Manage iCloud app data through Apple’s storage settings. Contact us for help understanding the available controls; the publisher cannot retrieve your private CloudKit content.

You can stop using optional voice, AI, spoken-task and system features without submitting task information to the publisher. iCloud sign-in and storage are managed by Apple.

Support and this website

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and troubleshoot and retained as needed for the request or legal obligations. You may request deletion by email, subject to such obligations. Avoid including sensitive task or account information.

This site uses GitHub Pages with no added analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process technical requests to operate and secure hosting under its Privacy Statement.

Children and changes

Unread is a general-audience productivity app, not directed at children under 13. We may update this policy when app behavior changes; the effective date above identifies this version.
